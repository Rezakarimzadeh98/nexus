# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful and inclusive community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Focus on ideas, not individuals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or intimidation.
- Insults, hate speech, or discrimination.
- Deliberate disruption or spam.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content that violates this policy.
Repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent participation restrictions.

## Reporting

Open a private security-style issue or contact maintainers through repository contact channels.
