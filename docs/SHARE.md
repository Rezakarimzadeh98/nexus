Use this kit when posting to Show HN, Reddit, LinkedIn, X, or newsletters.  
Keep claims honest: Phase status must match [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). Never invent live metrics.

## Show HN

**Title**

`Show HN: NEXUS – living intelligence engine (state, signals, evidence, scored forecasts)`

**Post**

Core concepts stay stable; adapters plug domains (news, finance, cyber, …). Optional language models may help extract and explain — they are not the product.

## Reddit / LinkedIn / X

Lead with the human failure: *too much data, no living picture, no scorekeeping.*  
Then the loop image / live demo.  
Then one ask: star, Discussion with a domain, or try an adapter pack.
